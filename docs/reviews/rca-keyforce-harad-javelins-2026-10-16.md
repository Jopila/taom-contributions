# RCA: porting KEYforce's Harad javelins, maces and bardings (lotraom-assets 03320a65, 2026-10-16)

## Top-line

KEYforce's `03320a65` added Shaghana javelins, Harad one-handed maces, the regular-line bardings and saddles, his
cavalry sword on every cavalry tier, re-kitted Custom Battle lords and a Custom Battle troop filter. He committed the
TAOM half himself (`d94ee1631`, pushed), and his art was already live, so the port installed the Armory ModuleData (three
files verbatim, two three-way merged keeping the #805 edits), wrote 32 English rows, registered the Custom Battle name,
seeded 54 missing language rows per language, and reseeded the Shaghana and Abanissa enlistment kits.

`/deep-review` ran eight `deep-reviewer` lenses in two waves (1, 2, 5, 7; then 3, 4, 6, Tooling) and one convergence
pass. **No engine incompatibility**: the javelin and mace templates resolve, the bardings fit `Monster.horse`, and the
Custom Battle stylesheets load only for `CustomGame`. Two findings rated HIGH by the Tooling lens: one was this port's
(the starter-kit block, fixed here), one a tool defect already tracked in #812. Every finding is fixed in this change
or recorded below.

## Root-cause pattern

**A check that cannot fail looked like evidence twice.** The starter-kit `--verify` stays green when its block loses
someone else's rows (finding 1), and the baseline validator run reported "before" data it never read (finding 2). Both
were caught only because a reviewer asked what the check actually reads. The fixes make each check fail on the case:
the new test sets the starter-kit blocks aside, and the melee comparison now runs on two real troop trees.
