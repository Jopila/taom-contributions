# TAOM v2.0.35 (tester build)

## ⚠️ Bannerlord v1.5.5

This build is for **Bannerlord v1.5.5** (Steam **beta** branch; the last note said v1.5.3). All four modules (TAOM,
TAOM.Dependencies, TAOM_Map and LOTRLOME_Armory) read **v2.0.35**; install them together, because KEYforce's Harad
gear needs the matching Armory. If you already have the v2.0.35 testing package from 18 October, replace it: it showed
five "[Test]" WildMesh riders in the Custom Battle picker, and this build removes them.

- **Coming from the v2.0.34 or v2.0.33 tester build:** you can keep your campaign and still get the fixes, the balance changes
  and the new battles. You will not get Arthedain, Carn Dûm, Lithur or any new village or town (see below). A save
  that holds Harad troops from before the Harad tree rework of 10 October is not supported: start a new campaign
  for Harad.
- **Coming from v2.0.32 or older:** start a new campaign. The wild troll bands exist only in a campaign started
  with v2.0.33 or later.
- **A new campaign is needed for:** playing Arthedain (its towns sit at the end of the map's settlement list, so an
  older save drops them), Gundabad's Carn Dûm and Lithur with their villages, Luinkrag in Blue Craig, the
  Hoarwell Watch rename, Gondor starting with Land Grants for Veterans, and a world without Calradian wanderers.
- **Kept in an old save:** lords already generated keep their gear, wanderers already in the world stay until
  recruited, and your policies stay.
- The MCM "Dread Radius" setting is stored under a new name, so it starts at the new 5 m default once. Your other
  settings are untouched.

Everything in the v2.0.34 tester build is still here. What is new since then:

## 🏰 Arthedain and the far north (new)

- **Arthedain**, the North-kingdom of Arnor, is playable: King Arvegil II rules from Fornost Erain over five small
  clans and twenty lords, with three towns, seven castles and 24 villages. It is weak by design. Pick it at character
  creation (Ranger of the North, Warden of Fornost, Knight of Arthedain) or on the faction map. It has a 23-troop
  Dunedain tree in Arnor armour, and its lords now spawn with about 100 men instead of about 45.
- **Gundabad** gains **Carn Dûm** (a town held by the Morzûg clan, four villages) and **Lithur** (a castle, the
  Grishûk clan's first fief, villages Lith-bosh, Lith-mauz and Lith-mîr). Arthedain's castle_AN5 is now Hoarwell Watch.
- **Blue Craig** gains a second town, Luinkrag, with three villages.
- Arthedain's markets sell Arnor armour and Numenorean swords and poleaxes; Lindon's sell Rivendell's silver armour
  and weapons. The Arnor King's Crown is never sold, looted or won; King Arvegil II starts with it.

## ⚔️ War of the Ring

- **Peace first.** From day 0 to day 30 no kingdom is at war. On day 30 Isengard and Dunland attack Rohan; the full War
  of the Ring begins on day 44, when Gundabad also attacks Arthedain. Old start wars are gone. A war you cause
  yourself still happens, and the kingdom screen now says why a war is refused. In an old save the start wars end on
  the first day after loading.
- **The Full War now declares its wars.** Before, no Hostile-pair war was ever declared (no notice, no history entry).
  A save already in the full war is repaired when it loads.
- A new campaign no longer inherits the previous one's War of the Ring when you start it without restarting the game.
- **Eye of Sauron hunt:** a Ringwraith (Glorfindel if you play the Evil side) hunts a player whose deeds draw the Eye.
  Single-player only. Escaping his host does not end the hunt; he picks up your trail within the hour and rides faster
  once within one and a half times your sight. While you ride in another lord's army he holds off.
- **War Chronicle:** timed war effects, a rally for AI kingdoms that have lost 25% or 50% of their fortresses, and
  chronicle sieges with announcements. The rally and effects never help your own clan; the MCM groups "War of the
  Ring/Rally" and "War Effect Strength" scale or switch them off.
- **Balance:** the evil cultures' party-size feats are capped at 10% (Goblin-town and Blue Craig 40% to 10%, Misty
  Mountain Orcs 30% to 10%, Mordor, Isengard, Gundabad and Dol Guldur 20% to 5%), because a 200-day simulation had the
  free peoples falling from 47 to 41 men per 100 evil men. Evil garrisons shrink too (a Goblin-town town from about 560
  to 440). Armies pick new siege targets: Dunland goes for Rohan then Gondor, Gundabad for Mirkwood and Erebor.
- Stuck AI battles (for example 8000 against 0, with the whole faction standing still) now end.

## 🧙 Troops, gear and careers

- **Harad and Shaghana** carry KEYforce's spears, swords, one-handed maces, javelins, shields and bardings, with his
  [Harwan] and [Shaghana] names. The Serpent and Red Serpent marksmen get a tier-7 bow. Elephant and Mumakil riders
  are bought from the Elite Emissary at Korb Taskral (70 War Drums, 2,800), no longer volunteers.
- Uruk careers start in uruk armour, and the Crossbow careers and Harad javelin starts carry what they are named for.
- Lords the game creates (coming of age, rebels, new clans) dress in their own culture's kits, not Calradian gear.
- Lindon troops weigh and survive like Rivendell's again: its 22 elites take more party space, and its soldiers die
  40 percent less often when struck down. A Lindon AI party over its limit trims on its next upgrade check; a player's
  party is never trimmed.
- Career fixes: the tier lock and the one-keystone rule now work, a dimmed diamond says why in its tooltip, party-size
  picks apply at once and only to your own party, and a career gain now scales every earning, not only town income.
- Raids no longer pay the battle reward on top, and a send-troops hideout clear pays once.
- 53 wanderers of the goblin, orc, Blue Craig, Lindon and Arthedain cultures tell their own stories instead of an
  "ERROR: Text with id backstory_c" line. Dale and Khand get ten wanderers each.
- Tournaments keep the sides apart (no Mordor orcs at Minas Tirith; MCM Tournaments, "Keep Enemy Sides Out") and
  fighters wear their own armour.

## 🗡️ Battles and scenes

- **Orc gaits:** each orc and goblin soldier walks with its own step size and one of three gaits (a hunched limp and two
  variants). In a first Custom Battle 125 orcs split 38, 37 and 50. MCM group Animation Variety has the switches.
- **Dread aura:** it now reaches only enemies in contact (5 m, full within 2 m) and drains their morale to 10 without
  routing them.
- **Siege troop picker** (Manage Troops before each wall battle; trolls start unticked) and a **troll siege role**
  (trolls smash the gates instead of standing at ladders). MCM Battle Tactics / Siege Forces.
- **Custom Battle** now runs TAOM's combat rules (crush-through, shield penetration, signature strikes, horse charge).
- New scenes: Ruined Outpost and Pelennor Fields for Gondor, and Kysaro's Pelargir, Serelond, Eastern Moria, Mordor
  Town, Rohan Plains II, Amon Gelin and Lothgobel. Known: the five Mordor towns on the shared scene have no player
  spawn, so a night walk puts you outside the walls; some houses in Pelargir, Serelond and Lothgobel are missing.
- Formation presets in Order of Battle save and load (MCM "Enable Formation Presets", off by default).
- Dwarf portraits no longer sit too low in the frame.

## ⚡ Performance and stability

- No more quarter-second stall on the map when a castle or town falls (ButterLib's Distance Matrix is off).
- The map skips hidden settlement nameplates, and every 20th screen cover over the map releases map memory.
- A battle whose start throws now loads with one message instead of looping; a skeleton buffer guard stops a freeze
  in huge battles. MCM TAOM Crash Report.
- A window shows "Compiling shaders: 412 of about 1336" during a shader rebuild.
- A Fiefs button sits beside Kingdom on the map bar (same as F6).
- If troops vanish from your party for no reason, send taom_debug.log: it now logs each loss.

## 🌍 Translations and Bannerlord v1.5.5

- About 1,190 lines per language are translated into all 12 languages: the War Chronicle, the Eye of Sauron hunt and
  the race abilities among them. Text added after 14 October (the war-refusal lines, Carn Dûm and Lithur, the new
  scene names) shows in English for now.
- Vanilla v1.5.5 fixes come with it: weak lords no longer attack an army that has just raided, and incidents no longer
  delete captive lords. v1.5.4 also made low-security towns lose loyalty a little faster. The "Native Skin Fixes" MCM
  group is gone.
