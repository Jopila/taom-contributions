# TAOM v2.0.36 (tester build)

## ⚠️ Bannerlord v1.5.6

This build is for **Bannerlord v1.5.6** (Steam **beta** branch; the last note said v1.5.5). You must be on the v1.5.6
beta. The v2.0.35 build does not run correctly on v1.5.6: the howdah and mumakil platforms failed to load, and
prisoner escape, siege defender engines and forest sight threw errors. This v2.0.36 build does not run on v1.5.5.

- **Modules:** all four modules (TAOM, TAOM.Dependencies, TAOM_Map and LOTRLOME_Armory) read **v2.0.36**. Install
  them together.
- **Saves:** an existing v2.0.35 campaign should keep working. Report anything odd. Start a new campaign to play
  Cardolan or Rhudaur, and for Lindon's new havens, castles and villages, the extra Dale houses and the new faces
  for lords. Lords already in a save keep their old faces and clans, and the Lindon renames reach every save. The new
  Postern Gate building appears in an old save on its next load, at level 0.
- ⚠️ **Fourteen settings were reset to their intended values** (list under Fixes). Their labels are unchanged, but a
  value you set yourself is gone and must be set again.
- ⚠️ **"Enable Enlistment Trace"** (Enlistment, Diagnostics) and **"Show Battle Action Bar"** are stored under new
  names, so both start at their default (off) once.

Everything in the v2.0.35 tester build is still here. What is new since then:

## 🗺️ Bannerlord v1.5.6

- **Field parley** (new in v1.5.6) respects Hidden Hosts. A host the map shows as "Unknown party" has no parley icon
  or hint, and a click on it prints "Cannot parley with this party.", so a parley no longer reveals who leads it.
- **Turbo clicks work again.** v1.5.6 made Left Ctrl + click a parley, and TAOM's turbo is Ctrl + Space, so a turbo
  click on a town or party tried to parley instead of travelling there. While turbo is held, a click is an ordinary
  click. Known limitation: on the single frame where Space is pressed or released, a click can follow the old turbo
  state.
- An AI lord who leads an army has a "Last seen leading an army near ..." line on his encyclopedia page (English until
  the translation pass).
- The combat changes in the v1.5.6 patch notes (horse charge knockdown, head and neck pierce, horse and troop speeds,
  the Big Game Hunter and Feller perks) are multiplayer only. Campaign and Custom Battle behave as before.

## 🌫️ Hidden Hosts

- Another party's troop count on the map blurs with distance ("1??", then "12?", then the exact number). Near the
  edge of your sight a host reads "...", and its name shows as "Unknown party". A better scout, the lookout camp and
  your own realm's hosts sharpen it. AI lords still see true numbers. Tooltips blur the same way, and a hovered host
  does not show its orders until it has been counted. MCM: Map UI / Hidden Hosts.
- **Under the Eaves:** a party standing in forest sees 30 percent less far (a scout with Keen Sight halves that;
  settlements are exempt).

## 🛡️ Fiefs, sieges and armies

- **Keep Wardens:** castle patrols now matter, castles with a Guard House send out patrols of their owner's culture,
  and AI lords build Guard Houses first.
- **Postern Gate:** a new military project in towns and castles that cuts the troops you lose breaking into or out of a
  siege by 50, 67 and 75 percent over its three levels. MCM: Fief Management / Postern Gate.
- **From the Ashes:** your company can rebuild a looted village of your realm (24 hours, 20 healthy men, 40 hardwood).
- **Watch-Silver** buys fief loyalty from a governor. **Reeve's Ledger** turns the town window into a profit and
  projects ledger. **Namegiving:** a ruler can rename a fief for influence. Fief votes show what each clan holds.
- **Watchful Holds:** enemy forts now harry parties marching past them (disorganized, a few losses, a red message).
- **The Red Arrow:** you can call parties into your army while it besieges. **The King's Muster:** AI rulers pay half
  the influence to call a host. **Help Unlooked-For:** strong lords no longer flee a siege they can face. **Hold the
  Walls:** Free Peoples armies hold a taken fort for about a day.
- **Fire and Tribute:** in the full war, raiding hosts ride out to burn the villages of the strongest enemy realm. An
  envoy may come to you demanding tribute for your villages.
- AI lords train and learn faster (**Seasoned Hosts**) and skip recruiting trips that the Free and Evil rule would
  refuse. A culture's foot now makes a stand against a larger mounted army (Erebor, Blacklocks, Isengard, Dale).

## 🤝 Relations, lords and companions

- Relation can now be earned: **Bonds of Battle** (fighting beside lords, clearing bandits), **Good Deeds** (mercy and
  honor), **Tokens of Esteem** (gifts to lords and companions) and **Long Memory** (villages remember who raided them).
- **Bond of Hire:** a mercenary clan stays at least 30 days. **Sworn Anew:** a captured clan leader may agree to join
  your kingdom. **Hard Questions:** questioning a captive lord, with the clans it angers counted.
- **Lord Courtesies:** give a lord troops, and ask about his holdings. **Oaths of Service:** companions ask hire
  terms and have more to talk about. **Notables** can hand over men, introduce you and point out holds.
- **Messengers** now cost more with distance (25 denars a day of travel on top of the base) and no longer move armies
  or troops. A greyed Send Messenger button says why.
- Bandit clans of Dunland, Rhun, Harad, Gundabad, Umbar, Gondor deserters, the Blacklocks and Mirkwood speak in their
  own voices.

## ⚔️ Enlisted service

- Seven ranks now (Private to Sergeant Major); an old save keeps its place, pay and kit.
- A served term ends with a choice to sign on again or leave with honor. Lords remember past contracts for two terms.
  Desertion costs 15 relation and some Honor.
- The board shows what the column is doing, promotions get a notice, and you take spoils from foes you killed.
- A soldier's wars now follow his lord's, he fights the lords who relieve a raid, and stays out of the raids
  themselves. He can no longer walk out of the commander's battle.

## 🏕️ Camps, tavern and inventory

- Field camps gain camp work, rest healing and an optional start in camp. **Review the host** shows your army in
  formation on the ground. **Far Watch** sends a scout from camp. The **Provender-master** buys food, sells your loot
  at a nearby town and keeps the host fed.
- Tavern keepers sell a round for your company or the house. **Telchar's Mark** tints better gear in the inventory (and
  can hide the rest). **Bill's Burden** shows cargo fullness on the map bar.
- Smithing: hold Shift (five) or Ctrl (the whole stack) to smelt or refine in bulk. Smelting yields are capped.
- **Hammerhand:** a shield bash or kick can floor a man. **Fell Foes:** cornered routers turn and archers mark you.
- Other new screens: a family tree (**Lines of Descent**), the **Hearth Roll** warning for unhappy companions, a
  **Loremaster's Line** on the skill bar, banners on captured lords, hero filters by race and age in the encyclopedia,
  and screens that open without sliding in. Villager and AI lord trades show on town nameplates.
- Cultures: every playable culture, and Arthedain, Cardolan and Rhudaur, start careers in a kit of their own.

## 🏰 Realms and the map

- **Cardolan and Rhudaur**, the other two kingdoms of divided Arnor, join as Neutral realms with their own troop
  trees, lords and colours. Tharbad and Minas Eithel in Cardolan swap names.
- **Lindon** grows to three havens, three castles and 22 villages, with Harlond, Forlond and four new Elven houses.
  So that no name repeats, Gondor's Harlond castle is now Harlond Quays.
- **Dale** gains five houses and 27 lords (14 clans, 75 adult lords), each with a face of its own, and its castles are
  shared out again among its houses.
- Renamed villages get descriptions of their own, and Sauron has a face set for scene popups.

## 🔧 Fixes

- **Settings reset:** Initial Hideouts Per Faction 7 and Max Parties Per Hideout Cap 6; Auto-Knockdown Weight Ratio 6;
  Enable Formation Presets and Enable Smart Cavalry AI off; War of the Ring Phase 1 Start Day 30 and Phase 2 Start
  Day 44; Merit To Promote 32 and Offers Per Battle 2; and the AI Party Size multipliers back to neutral.
- **Archers no longer turn invisible** with Gondor steel, Dale, Noldor, Isengard, Harwan, Rhun or Mirkwood bows.
- Talking to a tavern musician no longer crashes in Dol Guldur, Isengard, Lothlorien, Rivendell, Goblin Town or Lindon.
- Rulers' coronation title no longer reads "ERROR: Text with id str_liege_title"; coronation guards fit the realm.
- Grammar in translations works again (possessives, articles, case endings), and 177 vanilla texts regain their
  translations. Portuguese and Italian names carry proper grammar.
- Portraits show clan colours; seven banners lose blank or debug layers; lords of TAOM cultures carry battle banners;
  Lindon and Blue Craig troops raise banners.
- Dunland Hunter and Falcon archers fight as archers. Arthedain troops cost no special resources. Voiced dialogue uses
  a fitting voice for TAOM cultures.
- Smaller fixes: the inventory doll keeps clan colours, presets refresh it, Unequip All updates the open list, tavern
  maids offer a dish in every town, a service menu always has a way out after discharge, and the map bar resource
  appears on the first frame.
- Banner sheets load compressed, using far less memory.
